Inner voices: revision rounds, a debate stance, and a per-turn mode the agent chooses within your bounds. A revision is reviewed again by both voices (independently) and the agent reflects again, up to `maxRevisions` (1–3, default 1); each round's hidden prompt has its own OTID (`<run>:reflect`, `:reflect2`, `:reflect3`) and history hides them all. `run.counsel.rounds` records each round's notes, outcome and the version it reviewed. New `debate` mode: Prudence prosecutes with specific, checkable objections only; Ambition defends (steelman, weak objections, how to strengthen); the agent may KEEP with a one-line rebuttal (`DEBATE_ADDENDA`, `voicePersona`, `rebuttalOf`). New always-allowed `inner_voices({ mode, reason })` tool (offered only with voices on and "Agent decides"): its last call sets the turn's mode, for that turn only, clamped to the person's minimum (`none`, `calibrated_if_tools`, `calibrated`, `debate`) and maximum (`calibrated`, `debate`); without a call the default mode applies (`VOICES_NOTE`, `clampVoiceChoice`, `innerVoicesTool`). Settings add `decides`, `defaultMode`, `minimum`, `maximum` and `maxRevisions` (validated: min ≤ max, default between); the old `speakAfter` migrates (`long` → minimum `calibrated_if_tools` + default `skip`; `every` → default `calibrated`). The app shows "Revised 2× after Ambition & Prudence", each round in the collapsed section, and the agent's choice ("Debate: …", "Skipped review: …").
