Inner voices, Ambition and Prudence (piece 1: on demand). Two optional advisory voices per agent, in the spirit of Jiminy and mirror images of each other: Ambition (confident, daring, wants success and recognition; looks for the upside, bigger goals, finishing and publishing) and Prudence (critical, wants to avoid mistakes and shame; sees weak evidence and risks, wants checks and smaller steps). Each states the other's legitimate point. They have no tools, give advice only and treat everything they read as untrusted data. `ai-sdk-letta` adds `counsel()` (both voices in parallel, 60 s each, one failing never fails the other), `lettaVoice` (a fresh hidden, tool-less, memory-less temporary agent per call, swept by `sweepVoices`), `AMBITION_PERSONA`, `PRUDENCE_PERSONA`, `VOICE_NOTE_SCHEMA` (`{ points[1..4], confidence, suggestion?, disagreeWorthDeciding? }`) and `counselReminder`. Jiminy and the voices now share `temporary-advisor.ts` (`askTemporaryAdvisor`, `chooseReviewerModel`); Jiminy behaves as before. Server: `POST /v1/threads/:id/runs/:runId/counsel` asks the voices about a finished turn (the message, the reply and a bounded tool summary), stores the notes with the run (`run.counsel`), returns them in the thread's view (`counsels`), and gives them to the agent once, as a labelled reminder in its next turn. Settings per agent (off by default; model `auto` picks another family than the agent's): `GET`/`PUT /api/adoption/agents/:id/voices` (stored in the adoption record) and `GET`/`PUT /api/voices` for the app's own agent (`<state>/server/<id>/voices.json`). Web: "Ask Ambition & Prudence" under finished replies when a voice is on; a collapsed "Ambition & Prudence weighed in ›" line opens both notes side by side (amber and blue; stacked on phones); a new "Inner voices" section in the agent settings.
