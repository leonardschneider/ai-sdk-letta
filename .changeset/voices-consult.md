Inner voices: the agent consults them mid-turn and can no longer skip them. The `inner_voices` mode tool is replaced by the always-allowed `consult_voices({ question, context?, mode?, voices? })` (`consultVoicesTool`, `CONSULT_VOICES_TOOL`): the agent chooses when, its question and the facts the voices see, the stance (clamped to the person's minimum and maximum) and which voice (both by default); the notes come back as the tool result and the turn continues (at most `CONSULT_LIMITS.perTurn` = 8 a turn). Every turn hears the voices: a turn that never consulted them (no consultation brought a note; `consultedVoices`) gets the end-of-turn review in the minimum stance. Settings are now `{ ambition, prudence, minimum, maximum, maxRevisions }` with `minimum`/`maximum` in `calibrated | debate`; earlier `decides`, `defaultMode` and `speakAfter` are accepted and dropped, and a minimum `none`/`calibrated_if_tools` becomes `calibrated`. `OpenAgentOptions.innerVoices` now returns a `VoiceConsultant`; `ThreadRuntime.innerVoicesBounds()` is replaced by `voiceConsultant()`; `RuntimeVoices.ask` takes an optional voice list. Removed: `innerVoicesTool`, `clampVoiceChoice`, `parseInnerVoices`, `VOICE_CHOICES`, `VOICE_MINIMUMS`, `minimumRank`, `INNER_VOICES_REASON_MAX` and their types. Fix: a reflection answering `KEEP.` (or `KEEP,`/`KEEP;`) followed by a rebuttal on the same line keeps the reply instead of showing that line as the revised reply; long one-line rebuttals are cut to 300 characters. The app shows each consultation as a collapsed "Asked Ambition & Prudence: <question>" line with both notes, and the settings drop "Who decides" and "Default mode".
