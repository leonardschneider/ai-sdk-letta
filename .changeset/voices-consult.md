Inner voices: the agent consults them mid-turn, and skips them only when the person's minimum allows. The `inner_voices` mode tool is replaced by the always-allowed `consult_voices({ question, context?, mode?, voices? })` (`consultVoicesTool`, `CONSULT_VOICES_TOOL`): the agent chooses when, its question and the facts the voices see, the stance (clamped to the person's minimum and maximum) and which voice (both by default); the notes come back as the tool result and the turn continues (at most `CONSULT_LIMITS.perTurn` = 8 a turn). Skipping is a separate, voluntary per-turn tool, `skip_voices({ reason })` (`skipVoicesTool`, `SKIP_VOICES_TOOL`), offered only when the minimum allows it: `none` (any turn, the default) or `calibrated_if_tools` (a turn without other tools); `calibrated`/`debate` never (`skippable`, `maySkip`). The agent's note depends on the minimum (`voicesNote`). A turn that neither consulted them (no consultation brought a note; `consultedVoices`) nor validly skipped is reviewed at its end in the minimum's stance (`floorMode`). Settings are now `{ ambition, prudence, minimum, maximum, maxRevisions }`; earlier `decides` and `defaultMode` are accepted and dropped, and `speakAfter: 'long'` becomes minimum `calibrated_if_tools`. `OpenAgentOptions.innerVoices` now returns a `VoiceConsultant`; `ThreadRuntime.innerVoicesBounds()` is replaced by `voiceConsultant()`; `RuntimeVoices.ask` takes an optional voice list. Removed: `innerVoicesTool`, `clampVoiceChoice`, `parseInnerVoices`, `VOICE_CHOICES`, `minimumRank`, `INNER_VOICES_REASON_MAX` and their types. Fix: a reflection answering `KEEP.` (or `KEEP,`/`KEEP;`) followed by a rebuttal on the same line keeps the reply instead of showing that line as the revised reply; long one-line rebuttals are cut to 300 characters. The app shows each consultation as a collapsed "Asked Ambition & Prudence: <question>" line with both notes, and the settings drop "Who decides" and "Default mode" (Minimum now reads "None (agent may skip)", "If tools used", "Calibrated (never skip)", "Debate (never skip)").
